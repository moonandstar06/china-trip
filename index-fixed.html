How to make a Google Photos shared album
  1. Open the Google Photos app.
  2. Tap your profile → Utilities → Start a shared album (or select photos → Share → Create shared album).
  3. Name it Asian Pandas 🐼 China and turn on Collaborate so others can add photos.
  4. Tap Share → Copy link.
  5. Paste that link above. Done! 🎉

Tip: since Google is blocked in China, keep your VPN on to upload photos in real time — or upload each night once you're back on hotel wifi + VPN.
